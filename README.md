## 📖 About Classora

**Classora** is a modern school management system that brings a school's daily administrative and academic work into one organized platform. Schools often depend on paper registers, spreadsheets, and scattered records, which leads to errors, delays, and wasted time. Classora replaces all of that with a single, secure, easy-to-use system.

It is built for four types of users: **admins, teachers, students, and parents**. Each one gets their own login and sees only the information relevant to them.

> 💡 *Spend less time on paperwork, more time on education.*

### 🎯 Objectives

- Reduce manual work and record-keeping errors
- Save time for teachers and administrative staff
- Give quick and secure access to school data
- Improve communication between school, students, and parents
- Help school leaders make decisions using clear reports

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧑‍🎓 Student Management
- Add and manage student admissions and enrollment
- Store personal details, guardian information, and contact numbers
- Assign students to classes and sections
- Keep a complete academic history for each student
- Search, filter, edit, and update records quickly

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher & Staff Management
- Maintain teacher and staff profiles and contact details
- Assign teachers to classes and subjects
- View each teacher's workload and schedule
- Track staff attendance

### 📅 Attendance Tracking
- Mark daily attendance for students in a few clicks
- Record staff attendance separately
- View attendance by day, month, class, or student
- Generate attendance summary reports
- Let parents and students check attendance anytime

### 🕒 Timetable Management
- Create class-wise and teacher-wise timetables
- Manage subjects, periods, and sections
- Avoid clashes between teachers and classes
- Students and teachers can view their timetable online

### 📝 Exam & Result Management
- Create exams and assign subjects and dates
- Enter marks subject-wise for each student
- Automatically calculate totals, percentages, and grades
- Generate printable report cards
- Track student performance over time

### 💰 Fee Management
- Set up fee structures for different classes
- Record fee payments and generate receipts
- Track pending and overdue fees
- View fee history for each student
- Give parents clear visibility of dues

### 📢 Notices & Communication
- Post school-wide or class-specific announcements
- Share events, holidays, and circulars
- Keep students and parents informed in one place
- Reduce dependence on paper notices

### 📊 Reports & Analytics
- Student performance and result reports
- Attendance summary reports
- Fee collection and pending dues reports
- Easy-to-read data that helps with planning

### 🔐 Role-Based Access & Security
- Separate logins for admin, teacher, student, and parent
- Each role sees only what it needs
- Password-protected accounts
- Protects sensitive student and staff data
